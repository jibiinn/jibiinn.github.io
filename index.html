Solar-Powered Public Address Board

An ESP32 board with a solar charger, battery protection and a regulated 3.3 V rail. It carries an I2S microphone and amplifier, GPS over UART, and USB-C programming through a CH340C.

  • Designed the full board in KiCad, including the LT3652 solar charging and Li-ion protection.
  • Laid it out for EMI/EMC: compact charger loop, antenna keepout, microphone kept apart from the amplifier and inductor, ground pours.
  • Cleared ERC and DRC against PCBPower design rules.

Custom ESP32-S3 Development Board

A development board built around the ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 module, with USB-C, an AMS1117-3.3 regulator, boot circuitry, a status LED, and GPIO and power headers.

  • Took it from schematic through component selection, footprint assignment, placement, routing and DRC.
